General-Specific

The Funnel Strategy: Start wide, end narrow.

A myriad of...

Quantifier

"A huge number of"

"There are a myriad of reasons why..."

Alter the course

Phrase

To change history/direction significantly.

To name a few

Connector

Used to introduce specific examples.

9-12月 Topic: Online Shopping

Q: Do you think people buy things they don't really need?

BLUEPRINT:

General: Start by saying "Without a doubt, consumerism is rampant..." + use phrase "A significant amount of people..."
↓
Specific: Give examples like live-streaming sales (TikTok/Douyin) or seasonal discounts.
↓
Conclusion: "So basically, impulse buying has become the norm."

9-12月 Topic: Scenery

Q: Is it important to take photos of beautiful scenery?

BLUEPRINT:

General: "Generally speaking, capturing moments is human nature..." + "A vast array of tourists..."
↓
Specific: Mention preserving memories or social media sharing.
↓
Pivot: "Having said that, some people focus too much on the photo and miss the actual view."

Pros & Cons

The Balance Sheet: Weighing the evidence.

Strike a balance

To find a compromise.

Boils down to

The essential point is...

On the flip side

Conversely / However.

9-12月 Topic: Public Parks

Q: What are the benefits of having parks in the city?

BLUEPRINT:

Pros: "On one hand, they act as the lungs of the city..." + "Provide space for recreation."
↓
Cons (Contrast): "On the flip side, maintaining them costs a fortune..."
↓
Balance: "It boils down to how the city manages its budget."

9-12月 Topic: Sharing Economy

Q: What are the pros and cons of sharing things with others?

BLUEPRINT:

Pros: "From a positive perspective, it fosters sustainability and reduces waste."
↓
Cons: "However, a significant drawback is hygiene concerns or wear and tear."
↓
Balance: "So, we need to strike a balance between being generous and being practical."

Classification

Divide & Conquer: Segment by age, geography, or role.

Tech-savvy

Adjective

Gen Alpha / Z: Good with technology.

Rat race

Idiom

The exhaustive struggle for financial success (Working Class).

Privileged

Adjective

Having special rights/advantages (Wealthy class).

9-12月 Topic: Gifts

Q: Do men and women like the same kinds of gifts?

BLUEPRINT:

Intro: "I wouldn't generalize, but we can look at this by group..."
↓
Group A (Stereotype): "When it comes to men, gadgets or utilitarian items might be preferred."
↓
Group B (Contrast): "In contrast, women might appreciate something with more sentimental value."
↓
Nuance: "But for Gen Z, these gender lines are blurring."

9-12月 Topic: News

Q: How do old people and young people get their news?

BLUEPRINT:

Group A (Elderly): "For the older generation (Gen X/Boomers), it's often traditional media like TV or newspapers."
↓
Group B (Youth): "However, for Gen Z and Millennials, they are tech-savvy and rely on social media feeds."
↓
Comparison: "The former seeks depth, while the latter seeks speed."

Time Travel

The Chronology: Past, Present, and Future.

Used to be

"It used to be the case that..." (Past habit)

Dramatic shift

A major change in trends.

Bound to

"It is bound to happen..." (Certainty in future)

9-12月 Topic: Housing

Q: How have houses in your country changed over the years?

BLUEPRINT:

Past: "Looking back 20 years, people used to live in spacious courtyards..."
↓
Present: "Nowadays, due to urbanization, there's been a dramatic shift to high-rise apartments."
↓
Future: "Looking ahead, smart homes are likely to become the standard."

9-12月 Topic: Advice

Q: Is the advice from older people always useful?

BLUEPRINT:

Opinion: "Not necessarily. I beg to differ with the idea that age equals wisdom."
↓
Analysis: "Despite their experience, their views might be outdated in this tech-driven era."
↓
Discussion (Conditional): "If it's about life values, yes. If it's about career/tech, maybe not."

9-12月 Topic: Team Sports

Q: Should children be encouraged to play team sports?

BLUEPRINT:

Opinion: "Absolutely. I completely resonate with this idea."
↓
Analysis: "The main reason is that it fosters collaboration and resilience."
↓
Extension: "Without these skills, they might struggle in the future workplace (Rat race)."
