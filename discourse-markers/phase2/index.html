Answer Architecture

Don't just learn phrases. Learn how to structure your answers so the words flow naturally.

01

Answer-Detail (AD)

Part 1 & Part 3 short answers

Answer Say yes/no or state your view directly
→
Detail Explain why with cause markers
→
Example Optional — give a concrete case
What I think is… Because… The reason is that… For instance…
Checklist: Used a logical connector? Paraphrased the question's verb? Avoided stacking information without linking words?
02

Y/N Question → ADE

For any Yes/No question in Part 1 or 3

YES Definitely! 100%. Absolutely. I'd say so.
NO Not really. I wouldn't say so. Probably not.
IT DEPENDS It depends. It could go either way. Well, it's not black and white.
Answer
→
Detail Why / How you feel
→
3rd Point On top of that + detail or example
Definitely / I'd say so On top of that… A great example is…
03

比较类 · Comparison

Which do you prefer? A or B?

Preference I'd go with… / …would be my preference
→
Compare Both not as…as / much more / far better
→
Justify Use degree adverbials + personal experience
Compared to… not as…as much/far/way + comparative
Checklist: Did I compare BOTH sides? Did I use not as…as naturally? Did I avoid only talking about the one I like?
04

总分-分情况 · General → By Situation

Do people in your country…? Is it common to…?

General StatementIt really depends on the situation
→
Situation AIn terms of [case A], …
→
Situation BWhen it comes to [case B], …
→
SummarySo basically, it varies
When it comes to… In terms of… The reason for this is… So basically…
05

总分-分群体 · General → By Group

How does this vary among different people?

General StatementThe impact varies significantly among different groups
→
Group 1Elderly people tend to be more…
→
Group 2Younger generations are generally…
→
ContrastHowever, there are exceptions…
Slice by: Age · Income · Urban/Rural · Job type · Interests · Education level
In terms of [group]… From [group]'s standpoint… However / In contrast… What stands out is…
06

总分-罗列 · General → List

What kinds of…? What are the benefits?

GeneralThere are plenty of… I'd mention
→
Point 1First, the most obvious one is…
→
Point 2On top of that, …
→
Point 3Last but not least, …
First / To begin with… On top of that / Secondly… Last but not least… A classic example is…
07

总分-强调 · General → Emphasize One

What's the most important…?

GeneralThere are several reasons, but if I had to pick…
→
EmphasizeWhat really matters here is…
→
SupportThis is largely due to the fact that…
→
ExampleA striking example of this is…
What … is … It all boils down to… If I had to choose one factor… The most prominent reason is…
08

Dialectical Discussion

Part 3 abstract questions · Show balanced thinking

General ObservationGenerally speaking, most people would say…
→
Specific AngleHowever, if we look more specifically…
→
Opposite ViewOn the flip side, some argue that…
→
SynthesisAt the end of the day, it's not black and white
Generally speaking… However / On the flip side… What it really comes down to… At the end of the day…
09

总分-举例 · General → Example

Quick P3 answers with impact

State ViewI believe that…
→
Striking ExampleA classic example of this is…
→
SignificanceThis shows that… / What this illustrates is…
A striking example is… What this illustrates is… That is to say…
