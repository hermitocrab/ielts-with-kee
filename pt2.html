Method: 5W1H Expansion + Natural Flow Markers
Stage 1: LEARN 🧠

Core Vocabulary: Relationships & Personality

Master these 9 keywords to describe friends and celebrities vividly. They will appear in the next section.

1. Go back a long way
/ɡəʊ bæk ə lɒŋ weɪ/
To have known each other for a long time.
We go back a long way, since primary school actually.
2. Down-to-earth
/ˌdaʊn tu ˈɜːθ/
Friendly, practical, and not arrogant.
Despite being top of the class, he is incredibly down-to-earth.
3. Crack jokes
/kræk dʒəʊks/
To tell jokes; to be funny naturally.
He has a great sense of humor and can crack jokes at any time.
4. On the same page
/ɒn ðə seɪm peɪdʒ/
Thinking in a similar way; agreeing.
When we study together, we are always on the same page.
5. Go viral
/ɡəʊ ˈvaɪrəl/
To become very popular very quickly on the internet.
His songs went viral on TikTok recently.
6. Catchy
/ˈkætʃi/
Pleasing and easily remembered (usually for music).
His melodies are so catchy and light-hearted.
7. Inspiring
/ɪnˈspaɪərɪŋ/
Giving you new ideas or making you feel you want to do something.
He is such an inspiring musician because of his talent.
8. Boost my mood
/buːst maɪ muːd/
To make someone feel happier.
Listening to his music really boosts my mood when I'm tired.
9. Distinctive feature
/dɪˈstɪŋktɪv ˈfiːtʃə/
A quality or characteristic that makes something easy to recognize.
He has a distinctive feature, which is a scar on his eyebrow.
Stage 2: TRY 🛠️

Natural Flow: The "5W1H" Podcast Method

Read the answers below. Notice how we use Discourse Markers to connect ideas logically (Logic Chain) and use the Vocabulary from Stage 1.

Scenario A: A Close Friend / Study Partner

(Topic: Describe a person you enjoy studying with or a close friend)

WHO
Background
Off the top of my head, the first person that pops into my mind is my best buddy, Michael. Basically, he’s a really nice guy who is very down-to-earth and energetic.
WHEN
WHERE
As far as I can remember, we go back a long way. We met when I was in primary school. I can't really recall the details, but I vaguely remember it was probably on a basketball court since we both love sports.
WHAT
Details
Specifically speaking, whenever we hang out, he always cracks jokes to make me laugh. On top of that, when we study together, we are always on the same page. He helps me with math, and I help him with English.
WHY
Feeling
As for the reason why I like him, I reckon that it's because of his positive attitude. To be honest, he can always put a smile on people's faces.
Scenario B: A Western Celebrity (New Gen)

(Topic: Describe a famous person you are interested in)

WHO
Identity
If you twist my arm to choose just one, I'd definitely say Charlie Puth. You know, he is one of the coolest and most inspiring singers nowadays.
WHAT
Features
Speaking of his appearance, he has a distinctive feature which is a scar on his eyebrow. Instead of hiding it, he embraces it. In terms of his career, his songs have totally gone viral on TikTok recently because the melodies are so catchy.
WHY
Feeling
The heart of the matter is that his music can always boost my mood. Whenever I feel "down in the dumps" (sad), I listen to his songs to cheer myself up. So, I am a huge fan.
Stage 3: APPLY 🚀

Real World Practice

Use the Vertical Timeline logic below to answer these real question bank topics. Don't forget the markers!

Topic 1: Describe a person you know who you are happy to spend time with.
You should say: Who this person is, How you know him/her, What you do together, and Explain why...
1. Opening (The "Pop-in"):
As soon as I saw this topic... (Mention the Friend from Scenario A).
2. Background (The "Time Traveler"):
If my memory serves me right... (Use "Go back a long way").
3. Activities (The "List Master"):
Apart from that... Plus... (Study, sports, travel).
4. Closing (The "Heart of Matter"):
All in all... I firmly believe that...
Topic 2: Describe a famous person you are interested in.
You should say: Who this person is, How you know about him/her, What he/she was like before famous, and Explain why...
1. Opening (The "Guess"):
Well, to be honest, I'm not a big fan of celebrities, but... (Mention Charlie Puth).
2. The Story (The "Fixer"):
Specifically speaking... (Talk about the eyebrow scar story/Dog bite accident).
3. The Impact (The "Ripple Effect"):
Due to the fact that... (His songs are catchy -> boost mood).
