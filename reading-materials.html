The Economist

Global news, politics, business, finance, science, and technology. The single most common source for IELTS and postgraduate exam reading passages. Sharp, concise prose with a distinct editorial voice.

The New Yorker

纽约客 - Weekly - Updated Saturdays

Long-form journalism, cultural criticism, fiction, and satire. Rich narratives and sophisticated vocabulary make it excellent for advanced reading comprehension and literary analysis.

The Atlantic

大西洋月刊 - Monthly - Updated 2nd of each month

Politics, culture, technology, and ideas. Known for deep-dive essays and intellectual rigor. A staple source for high-level reading comprehension and critical thinking practice.
