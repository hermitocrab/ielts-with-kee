🔬 The Lycurgus Cup is a beautifully decorated glass cup that was made in Italy in the fourth century. 🏺🇮🇹
For years, it confused scientists because it seems to change colour. 🤔🟢🔴
When light shines through it from the front, it looks green, but when it shines from the back, it appears red. 🟢→🔴
In the 1990s, scientists looked at the glass under a microscope. They discovered something amazing. 🔬✨
Whoever had made the glass had used tiny pieces of gold and silver. 🪙✨
Some of these pieces measured 50 nanometres across — about one-thousandth the size of a piece of salt. 🧂🔬
The Lycurgus Cup shows that the ancient Romans were using nanotechnology more than 1,600 years ago. 🧪🏆
⚡ In 1936, during some excavations in an ancient village near Baghdad, an archaeologist found some unusual clay pots. 🏺🏜️
The pots were more than 2,000 years old. 😱🕰️
Hanging inside each pot was a copper cylinder and inside this cylinder was a piece of iron. 🔩🟤
Nobody knew why somebody had made these pots with metal inside, but the archaeologist thought that they could be some sort of early battery. 🤔🔋
A few years later, American scientists made copies of the pots. They discovered that the original theory was right: they could make electricity. ⚡✅
But why? What did people use this electricity for? That is the great mystery of this discovery. 🕵️❓
🐉 More than 2,000 years ago, an inventor called Zhang Heng built the world's first earthquake detector. 🏮🇨🇳
The machine was a large metal vase on which there were eight dragon heads. 🐉🐉🐉🐉
The dragons marked the eight points of a compass, and each one had a metal ball in its mouth. 🧭⚽
Below each dragon head, there was a metal frog. 🐸
When the machine detected tremors in the earth, one of the dragons (facing in the direction of the tremor) would drop its ball into the mouth of the frog that sat below it. 📳🔔
This made a loud noise that warned people of the earthquake. 🔔⚠️
The machine could detect earthquakes hundreds of kilometres away. 🌍📏
📅 Very early calendars from ancient civilizations have been found in many countries around the world. 🌍📆
But archaeologists believe that they have discovered a calendar in Scotland that is 10,000 years old. 🏴󠁧󠁢󠁳󠁣󠁴󠁿😱
It was made by Stone Age Britons, who used it from 8000 BCE to 4000 BCE. 🪨👨‍👩‍👧
The calendar was made of 12 pits dug into the ground. 🕳️🕳️🕳️
The pits represented the months of the year and were laid out in a long line that was 50 metres long. 📏📅
The Ancient Britons would measure time using the sun and the moon. ☀️🌙
This ancient calendar used both to keep track of the days, weeks and months of the year. 📅✅

How to write about an invention:
① INTRO Say what it is. Name the invention and where/when it comes from. Every paragraph starts here!
② FACTS Give cool details. Dates, numbers, how it works, what it looks like. This is the juicy part!
③ IMPACT Tell us why it matters. What changed because of it? Why should we care?
