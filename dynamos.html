What is DynamOS?

DynamOS is not an LMS. Not a course library. Not a test-prep platform. It is a Competence Operating System — an AI-native platform that builds learner agency by turning real-world logistics into the curriculum itself.

"The product isn't courses. It's the ability to navigate real-world systems in a target language and culture — with AI as the scaffold and human teachers as expert guides."

— 🐺 003 + 🦄 002, Council Round 15

Three Engines Under One Roof

Inspired by reverse-engineering 飞象老师AI and our own pedagogical expertise.

🧠

Push/Pull Dual Engine

Pull: learner states intention → system scaffolds a path. Push: GAP analysis surfaces gaps the learner didn't know existed. Chinese doesn't have articles? Here's a targeted mission before you need it.

🎯

MFP/CCC/GAP Evaluation

SrKeeda's pedagogical framework encoded into AI. Meaning/Form/Pronunciation scoring. Collocation/Contexts/Connotation task design. Cross-linguistic GAP analysis as Push engine fuel.

🦖

DynaSaurus AI Tutor

Rebuilt around a competence graph. Persistent session memory. MFP-calibrated feedback. Seamless teacher handoff when AI hits its limit. Students feel DynaSaurus getting smarter, not a platform switch.
