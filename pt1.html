🗣️ Model Answers — Hometown (Most Tested)

Q: Where is your hometown?

Template: Location + Character + Personal Touch
My hometown is Chengdu, which is the capital of Sichuan province in southwestern China. It's famous for its spicy cuisine and laid-back lifestyle. I've lived there my whole life, so it holds a special place in my heart.

Q: What do you like about your hometown?

Template: Highlight 1 + Highlight 2 + Personal Connection
There are quite a few things — first of all, the food is absolutely incredible. You can find a huge variety of dishes, from street snacks to fancy hotpot. On top of that, the people here are incredibly warm and welcoming. It's just got this relaxed vibe that makes you feel at home right away.

Q: Has your hometown changed much?

Template: Big Picture + Specific Change + Personal View
Yeah, tremendously — it's like a completely different city from ten years ago. The skyline has transformed with new skyscrapers, and the metro system has expanded massively. I'd say it's mostly positive — more convenient, more opportunities — but sometimes I miss the old-school charm it used to have.
