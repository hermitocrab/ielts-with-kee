The Jump: What It Actually Takes突破：到底需要什么

Moving up a band isn't about studying harder. It's about studying the right thing.提分不是靠更努力地学，而是学对的东西。

5.0 → 5.5

Fix Your Foundation打好基础

At this level, grammar errors are your biggest bottleneck. Subject-verb agreement, tense consistency, article use — these basics cost you marks across all four skills. Expand your active vocabulary by 300–500 words focused on common IELTS topics (education, environment, technology, health). You don't need complexity yet. You need control.这个阶段，语法错误是最大瓶颈。主谓一致、时态一致性、冠词使用——这些基础问题在四项技能中都在扣你的分。围绕常见雅思话题（教育、环境、科技、健康）扩展 300–500 个主动词汇。你还不需复杂度，你需要控制力。

Typical timeline: 2–3 months of focused study典型时间线：2–3 个月专注学习
5.5 → 6.0

Add Coherence建立连贯性

Your ideas are fine — the problem is how you connect them. Learn to structure answers with topic sentences, supporting points, and examples. For speaking, reduce hesitation by practising extended turns — record yourself speaking for 2 minutes without stopping. The goal is flow, not perfection. For writing, master paragraph structure: one idea, three supporting sentences, one link to the next paragraph.你的想法没问题——问题在于怎么把它们连起来。学会用主题句、支撑点和例子来组织回答。口语方面，通过练习长段发言来减少犹豫——录下自己不停说 2 分钟。目标是流畅，不是完美。写作方面，掌握段落结构：一个观点，三句支撑，一句过渡到下一段。

Typical timeline: 2–4 months of structured practice典型时间线：2–4 个月结构化练习
6.0 → 6.5

Build Precision建立精准度

This is where "good enough" stops being good enough. Your vocabulary needs to be more precise — "important" becomes "crucial," "significant," "pivotal," or "paramount" depending on context. Collocation becomes critical: you stop saying "make research" and start saying "conduct research." In writing, your arguments need depth — two supporting points per paragraph, not one. In speaking, you stop translating from your first language and start thinking in English directly.这是"够好了"不再够用的阶段。你的词汇需要更精准——important 根据语境变成 crucial、significant、pivotal 或 paramount。搭配变得关键：你不再说 make research，而是 conduct research。写作上，论证需要深度——每段两个支撑点，不是一个。口语上，你不再从母语翻译，而是直接用英语思考。

Typical timeline: 3–5 months with targeted feedback典型时间线：3–5 个月有针对性的反馈
6.5 → 7.0

Develop Flexibility培养灵活性

The hardest jump in IELTS. Band 7 requires you to demonstrate range — not just accuracy. You need to show the examiner you can say the same thing three different ways and pick the best one for the context. Idiomatic language must sound natural, not memorised. Your writing needs sophistication — not just logically ordered, but elegantly structured. Your speaking needs confidence — not the absence of mistakes, but the ability to recover from them without losing flow. This is where most students plateau. The difference between 6.5 and 7 isn't more vocabulary — it's better access to the vocabulary you already have.雅思中最难的一跃。7 分要求你展示广度——而不只是准确度。你需要向考官展示你能用三种不同方式说同一件事，并选出最适合语境的那种。习语必须听起来自然，而非背诵。写作需要精湛——不只是逻辑有序，而是结构优雅。口语需要自信——不是没有错误，而是犯错后能不中断语流地恢复。这是大多数学生卡住的地方。6.5 和 7 的区别不是更多词汇——而是更好地调用你已有的词汇。

Typical timeline: 4–8 months of intensive, guided practice典型时间线：4–8 个月高强度有指导的练习
